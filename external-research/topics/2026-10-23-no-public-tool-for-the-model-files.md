# No public tool for Hard Reset's model files (searched 2026-10-23)

**Why it matters here:** the dossier (2026-10-16) says the game's model files have no tools anywhere, which is why our
VR hands are drawn by our own add-on instead of being put into the game. This pass checked that again in public.

**What was found** `[reported]`:

- The only public modding write-up found is the *Exploit* mod's news post on ModDB ("Breaking ground in Hard Reset
  modding"). Its team first made a Hungarian translation, through which they learnt to change **fonts, text,
  animatix (the HUD's animated screens) and textures**, and then experimented with assets cut from the final game.
  It names no tool and no model or mesh format, and says the mod is "coming (very) soon"; no release page was found.
  <https://www.moddb.com/mods/exploit/news/breaking-ground-in-hard-reset-modding> (the page refused an automated
  read; these facts are from search excerpts).
- Hard Reset Redux moved to the studio's newer engine version (RoadHog v2, shared with Shadow Warrior 2), so a tool
  for Shadow Warrior 2 assets might one day reach Redux; nothing was found for the original game `[hypothesis]`.
  <https://pcgamer.com/hard-reset-redux-is-on-the-way>

**So:** the dossier's line still holds as far as public sources go `[reported 2026-10-23, two searches]`. Drawing our own
hands stays the route. The HUD's animatix files being editable by others fits what our own reader found
(`hud\hud3d.aix`, dossier 2026-10-17).
